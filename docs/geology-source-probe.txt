This is an outline to the code, data and maps provided here for the article "Global threat of arsenic in groundwater"

REFERENCE:
Podgorski, J., and M. Berg (2020), Global threat of arsenic in groundwater, Science, 368(6493), 845–850, doi:10.1126/science.aba1510.

Contact: joel.podgorski@eawag.ch, michael.berg@eawag.ch

Code:
random_forest_modeling.r
    - main script for
        (i) random forest variable selection, and
        (ii) full random forest model
    - calls functions.r and variables.r
    - relies on the R libraries pROC, randomForest and varSelRF 
functions.r
    - contains functions used in random_forest_modeling.r
variables.r
    - specifies the variables to use in modeling, which are contained in arsenic_concentrations_predictor_data.csv
    
Data:
arsenic_concentrations_predictor_data.csv
    - contains the geographical coordinates and arsenic concentrations averaged to 1km2 pixels from the sources listed in the paper's table S1
    - contains the values of the predictor variables as listed in table S2 corresponding to each arsenic point
groundwater_utilization_by_country.csv
    - contains the proportion of municipal water usage coming from unmanaged groundwater sources for rural and urban areas taken from the latest country-level reports provided by the UNICEF/WHO Joint Monitoring Program (JMP)
    - where JMP data were not available, the country-level groundwater withdrawal rates provided in FAO's AQUASTAT database were used (no distinction between rural and urban areas)
    
Maps:
    - probability_arsenic_gt_10ppb.tif
        geoTiff of probability of arsenic in groundwater >10 ppb (Fig. 2, main result of paper)
    - potentially_affected_population_0.57_threshold.tif
        geoTiff of potentially affected population at the 0.57 probability level, based on the 10 ppb probability map (Fig. 4)
    - potentially_affected_population_0.72_threshold.tif
        geoTiff of potentially affected population at the 0.72 probability level, based on the 10 ppb probability map (Fig. 4)
    - probability_arsenic_gt_5ppb.tif
        geoTiff of probability of arsenic in groundwater >5 ppb (fig. S9)
    - probability_arsenic_gt_50ppb.tif
        geoTiff of probability of arsenic in groundwater >50 ppb (fig. S10)

These maps are called Africa Groundwater Atlas country hydrogeology maps (Version 1.0, 2019/2021). 
They are digital maps in shapefile format.

The maps are the product of the Africa Groundwater Atlas project. 
The Africa Groundwater Atlas is an online resource that provides a summary of the groundwater resources of 51 African countries, and a gateway to further information. 
The Africa Groundwater Atlas is available at: https://www.bgs.ac.uk/africagroundwateratlas/index.cfm

The Africa Groundwater Atlas project was jointly funded by DFID, NERC and ESRC; supported by the International Association of Hydrogeologists (IAH); and carried out by the British Geological Survey (BGS) in partnership with hydrogeologists across Africa. 
It is a part of the Unlocking the Potential of Groundwater for the Poor (UPGro) Research Programme, which focused on improving the evidence base around groundwater availability and management in sub-Saharan Africa, to enable the sustainable use of groundwater that benefits the poor.
